# A-Level Mathematics Revision Notes: Differentiation

## 1. Lesson overview
* **Topic:** Introduction to differentiation.
* **Core Function Studied:** $y = 3x^2 + 4x - 5$
* **Main Takeaway:** Introduction to the power rule for differentiating polynomial expressions to find the derivative $\frac{dy}{dx}$.

---

## 2. Key mathematical concepts
* **Differentiation:** The process of finding the derivative, which represents the rate of change of a function.
* **Notation:** The derivative is denoted as $\frac{dy}{dx}$.
* **The Power Rule:** To differentiate a power of $x$, multiply by the power and reduce the power by one.
* **Constant Rule:** The derivative of a constant term (a number with no $x$ variable) is zero.

---

## 3. Worked examples
* **Example:** Differentiate the function $y = 3x^2 + 4x - 5$ with respect to $x$.

**Step-by-step solution:**
1. Identify the first term: $3x^2$. Multiply by the power ($2$) and reduce the power by $1$ ($2 - 1 = 1$), giving:
   $$3 \times 2x^{1} = 6x$$
2. Identify the second term: $4x$ (which is $4x^1$). Multiply by the power ($1$) and reduce the power by $1$ ($1 - 1 = 0$), noting that $x^0 = 1$, giving:
   $$4 \times 1 = 4$$
3. Identify the third term: $-5$ (a constant). The derivative of a constant is:
   $$0$$
4. Combine all the differentiated terms together:
   $$\frac{dy}{dx} = 6x + 4$$

---

## 4. Important formulae
* **The Power Rule for Differentiation:**
  $$\frac{d}{dx}(ax^n) = anx^{n-1}$$
* **The Constant Rule:**
  $$\frac{d}{dx}(c) = 0 \quad \text{(where } c \text{ is a constant)}$$

---

## 5. Common errors
* Forgetting to reduce the power by one after multiplying by the original power.
* Incorrectly differentiating linear terms like $4x$ to $4x$ instead of reducing it to just the constant $4$.
* Treating constant terms (like $-5$) as having a non-zero derivative instead of setting them to zero.
